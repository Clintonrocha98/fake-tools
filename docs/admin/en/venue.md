# Venue

The Venue section is the **scenario remote control** for the fake Binance sandbox. The happy path (fiat credit, spot fill, withdraw completion) advances on its own — nothing here needs a click for it to work. These pages exist for the failure scenarios the consuming application needs to exercise on demand: forcing a status, delaying a brcode, freezing an order, or flipping a global switch that makes every endpoint misbehave.
